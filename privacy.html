Snapper ENTERPRISE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Snapper AI Firewall browser extension and web application ("Snapper"), operated by McKinley Labs LLC ("we", "us"), collect, use, and protect your information.

What Snapper Does

Snapper is a security tool that monitors interactions between users and AI services (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others) to enforce organizational security policies. The browser extension intercepts content sent to AI services, scans for sensitive data, and enforces rules configured on your organization's Snapper server.

Data Collected by the Browser Extension

The Snapper browser extension collects the following data:

  • AI service interactions: Text submitted to supported AI services is scanned locally in your browser for PII patterns and policy violations before being sent. The text content itself is not transmitted to Snapper servers unless a rule evaluation is triggered.
  • Rule evaluation requests: When a policy check is needed, the extension sends the tool name, command, and relevant metadata to your organization's Snapper server for evaluation. The server returns an allow, deny, or approval-required decision.
  • AI service visit tracking: If shadow AI tracking is enabled, the extension records which AI services you visit (domain names and timestamps only, not page content). This helps organizations understand which AI tools are in use.
  • Clipboard monitoring: If enabled, the extension scans text pasted into AI services for PII patterns. Clipboard content is scanned locally and is not transmitted unless it triggers a rule evaluation.
  • Device information: Platform, browser type, timezone, and a generated device identifier are sent during registration so your organization can manage enrolled devices.
  • Authentication tokens: Your sign-in credentials are exchanged for session tokens stored locally in chrome.storage.local.

Data Collected by the Web Application

The Snapper web application (self-hosted by your organization) stores:

  • Audit logs: Records of all rule evaluations, including agent identity, command, tool name, decision, and timestamp.
  • PII vault entries: Sensitive values you explicitly store are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database. Encryption keys are derived from your organization's secret key.
  • Agent and device registrations: Names, identifiers, and configuration for registered AI agents and browser extension devices.
  • User accounts: Email, username, hashed password, and organization membership.

Where Data Is Stored

Snapper is a self-hosted application. All data is stored on infrastructure controlled by your organization. McKinley Labs LLC does not operate a cloud service and does not have access to your Snapper server, your audit logs, your PII vault, or any data processed by the extension.

The browser extension stores configuration and cached decisions locally in chrome.storage.local. No data is sent to any third party. All network communication is between the extension and your organization's Snapper server.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. The extension communicates only with the Snapper server URL configured by you or your organization's administrator. No analytics, telemetry, or tracking services are embedded in the extension or web application.

Data Retention

Data retention is controlled by your organization's Snapper administrator. Audit logs, PII vault entries, and device registrations can be deleted at any time through the Snapper dashboard or API. The browser extension's local storage can be cleared by removing the extension or using Chrome's "Clear browsing data" feature.

Your Rights

Because Snapper is self-hosted, your data rights are governed by your organization's policies. You can:

  • View your stored data through the Snapper dashboard
  • Delete PII vault entries at any time
  • Remove your device registration
  • Uninstall the browser extension to stop all data collection
  • Contact your organization's Snapper administrator for data export or deletion requests

Security

Snapper uses industry-standard security practices including AES-256-GCM encryption for PII vault entries, HKDF key derivation, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication with short-lived tokens, and HTTPS for all server communication. The extension enforces a configurable fail-safe mode (closed by default) when the server is unreachable.

Children's Privacy

Snapper is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
