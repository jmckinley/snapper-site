Snapper ENTERPRISE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy describes how Snapper, operated by McKinley Labs LLC ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information. Snapper is offered in three forms, and the data practices differ for each:

  • Hosted service — the closed-beta application we operate at app.snapperprotect.com, plus this marketing site (snapperprotect.com).
  • Self-hosted deployment — Snapper installed and operated entirely on your own infrastructure.
  • Browser extension — the client that intercepts AI-service traffic in your browser.

Hosted Service (Closed Beta)

When you use the hosted service at app.snapperprotect.com, McKinley Labs LLC operates the infrastructure and therefore acts as a data processor for the following:

  • Account data: email, username, hashed password, organization name, and membership.
  • Waitlist / access requests: when you request beta access from this site, we store the email, use-case description, and marketing-attribution parameters (UTM source/medium/campaign, referrer, landing page) you submit, along with your IP address and browser user-agent. We use this solely to send you a beta invite and to understand which channels our beta interest comes from. We do not sell it.
  • Audit logs & evaluation records: records of rule evaluations (agent identity, command/tool metadata, decision, timestamp) generated by your agents.
  • PII vault entries: sensitive values you explicitly store are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database; encryption keys are derived from your organization's secret key.

Hosted-service data is stored on cloud infrastructure we operate. We use a small number of infrastructure sub-processors — our hosting provider, and, only where you enable AI-assisted features for your organization, the AI model providers you configure. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Self-Hosted Deployments

When your organization installs Snapper on its own infrastructure, all data stays on infrastructure your organization controls. McKinley Labs LLC does not operate that server and does not have access to your audit logs, PII vault, or evaluation data. In that model your organization is the data controller and this policy's hosted-service section does not apply.

Browser Extension

The Snapper browser extension monitors interactions between you and AI services (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) to enforce your organization's policies. It collects:

  • AI service interactions: text submitted to supported AI services is scanned locally in your browser for PII and policy violations; the content is not transmitted unless a rule evaluation is triggered, in which case tool name, command, and relevant metadata are sent to the configured Snapper server.
  • AI service visit tracking: if shadow-AI tracking is enabled, the extension records which AI services you visit (domain + timestamp only, not page content).
  • Clipboard monitoring: if enabled, text pasted into AI services is scanned locally for PII; content is not transmitted unless it triggers a rule evaluation.
  • Device information: platform, browser type, timezone, and a generated device identifier, sent at registration so your organization can manage enrolled devices.
  • Authentication tokens: stored locally in chrome.storage.local.

The extension communicates only with the Snapper server URL you or your administrator configure — which may be the hosted service or a self-hosted server. No analytics or third-party tracking is embedded in the extension.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share data only with the sub-processors needed to operate the hosted service (listed above), and only as required by law. Self-hosted deployments and the extension communicate solely with the Snapper server you configure.

Data Retention

For the hosted service, waitlist entries are retained until the closed beta ends or you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. Account, audit, and vault data are retained for the life of your account and deleted on request. For self-hosted deployments, retention is entirely controlled by your organization's administrator.

Your Rights

You can request access to, export of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us (hosted service) or your organization's administrator (self-hosted). You can also uninstall the browser extension at any time to stop all extension data collection. If your organization requires a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), contact us and we'll provide one.

Security

Snapper uses AES-256-GCM encryption for PII vault entries, HKDF key derivation, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication with short-lived tokens, a tamper-evident audit hash chain, and HTTPS for all server communication. The extension enforces a configurable fail-safe mode (closed by default) when the server is unreachable.

Children's Privacy

Snapper is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
